How to play VIBE

On the device

  • PLAY / STOP start the beat, REC records pads and keys into the pattern.
  • Pads play drums – centre = loud, edge = soft.
  • Keys play the synth, swiping across works too.
  • Encoders: drag or scroll. A / B and DATA do what the display's left column says. Values show in the display.
  • F1–F6 do what the display shows above them.
  • MENU shows every encoder, key and pad of the open page in the display – hold SHIFT for the second layer.
  • Cheat sheet (top bar): how the running firmware is played, right of the device, toggles on and off.
  • OberVave 2.0 (default): four tracks A–D. F1 MIXER (again: LEVEL → PAN → SENDS), F2 TRACKS, F3/F4 sound, F5 SAVE, F6 GLOBAL, SHIFT+F6 UNDO. Colour keys FX ENV LFO ARP EDIT SEQ; SEQ again = TRACKS → STEPS → TRACK. Pad row 1 = start/stop a track on the bar, row 2 = select a track; in MIXER rows 2–3 mute (SHIFT+pad = solo). A = track, B = sound, DATA = bar (STEPS) or next pattern (MIXER / TRACKS). Hold REC 1 s or SHIFT+REC clears the selected track.
  • VIBE GROOVE 1.5 (groovebox): eight tracks A–H in two banks (tap SHIFT = A–D / E–H), each DRUMS or SYNTH with any kit or sound. Played like OberVave – same F keys, colour keys, pads and encoders.
  • OberVave 3 (beta) and GROOVE: the colour keys run as the signal goes – SOUND · ENV · LFO · ARP · FX · SEQ (OberVave 2.0 still FX ENV LFO ARP EDIT SEQ).
  • Holding with the mouse: in OberVave first SHIFT+F5 (HOLD), then a colour key – it stays held until you click it again.

Computer keyboard

  • ZXC… white keys, SDG… black keys (on a German layout Y instead of Z)
  • 1–8, Q–I pads 1–16
  • Space play/stop · O REC · Tab HOME
  • ←→ octave · Shift SHIFT · Esc back

Controllers (USB MIDI, Chrome/Edge)

  • Launchpad Mini MK3: top left 4×4 = pads, top right = PLAY/STOP/REC/HOME and the six keys, bottom two octaves of keys, lights as on the device.
  • MPK mini IV / MK3 (any preset, DAW too): K1–K4 = encoders 1–4, K5 = DATA, K6 = volume, K7 = A, K8 = B; pads = pads 1–8, bank B = 9–16; keys, pitch/mod wheel and sustain pedal go to the firmware. Buttons (only in the DAW preset): − / + step through the colour keys, MIXER, TRACKS, GLOBAL and MENU; TAP TEMPO = the chosen one again (pages through its area); Stop/Play = PLAY, Loop = STOP, Record = REC, Undo = HOME, Overdub = ENTER, SHIFT = SHIFT, push encoder = DATA. Black keys wrong? The MPK's Scales mode is on (SHIFT+pad 7 turns it off). Map it differently: Setup.
  • MIDI channel (top bar): "all" passes every channel to the firmware; channel 1–16 only that one. Pads on channel 10 and the controller mappings (MPK, Launchpad, learned) always apply.
  • BeatStep Pro & co.: drum pads (channel 10) = pads; knobs with Learn: tap a VIBE control, move one on the controller.

Rear panel (below the device)

  • IN 1 / IN 2: channels 1 and 2 of your interface. LINE / MIC / HI-Z is set by the firmware.
  • PEDAL: footswitch (P or MIDI CC64) or expression (knob or CC11).
  • CV OUT / GATE OUT show what the firmware sends. CV IN: knob −5…+5 V. CLK IN: tap or MIDI clock.

Firmware

  • Choose it in the top bar, HOME + pad 16, or drop your own .wasm onto the device.
